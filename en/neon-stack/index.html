Neon Stack

One tap, one floor: stack glowing blocks to the finish line, chain perfect drops, and topple a guardian every five levels.

App Store · coming soon Google Play · coming soon Play in your browser

What's waiting for you

Tap. Slice. Perfect.

Line up each sliding block: the overhang is cut away, and perfect drops snap in with a rising chime.

No two levels alike

Wind, fog, drifting bases, pulsing blocks and bonus gems combine into a new challenge every level.

Guardians every 5 levels

Dodge the Pendulum's blade, outrun the Vice and outsmart the Inverter: only perfect drops do damage.

It gets serious fast

Faster blocks, taller towers, a thinner margin for error: each level asks a little more. Game over? You restart from the level after the last boss you beat.

Free, no interruptions

Neon Stack is ad-supported, but an ad never shows while you play: only between levels. No account needed.
