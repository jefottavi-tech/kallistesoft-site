Neon Pop

Aim, bounce and pop your way through glowing boards. Calm and clever: every level has its own shape, and a bubble boss waits every fifth level.

App Store · coming soon Google Play · coming soon Play in your browser

What's waiting for you

Every level is new

Hearts, spirals, crowns and spinning wheels, each with its own colours and surprises.

Bombs, ice and rainbows

Special bubbles shake up every board, so you have to think before you shoot.

A boss every five levels

Peel away the shell of a giant bubble creature and hit it right in the eye.

It gets serious fast

More colours, fewer shots, a ceiling that drops faster: each level asks a little more. Game over? You restart from the level after the last boss you beat.

Free, no interruptions

Neon Pop is ad-supported, but an ad never shows while you play: only between levels. No account needed.
