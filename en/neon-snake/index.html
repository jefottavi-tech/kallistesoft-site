Neon Snake

A neon snake on a neon grid: grow fast, turn faster, and take down a boss every five levels.

App Store · coming soon Google Play · coming soon Play in your browser

What's waiting for you

New arena every level

Pillars, mazes, portals, moving walls, wraparound edges and arenas that close in as you eat.

Bosses every 5 levels

Grab a charge orb and ram the Wyrm, the Sentinel, the Brood Queen or the Pulsar before they get you.

Tense, fair, fast

Buffered swipes for split-second double turns, rival snakes stealing your food, and a checkpoint after every boss.

It gets serious fast

Faster, longer, more obstacles and rivals: each level asks a little more. Game over? You restart from the level after the last boss you beat.

Free, no interruptions

Neon Snake is ad-supported, but an ad never shows while you play: only between levels. No account needed.
